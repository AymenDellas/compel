A coaching website rebuild works best when both sides understand what is being improved and why. Before choosing a studio, look beyond the headline promise and ask how the proposed work fits your business.

Commercial terms are discussed with the scope of the proposed work. The questions below help make that discussion concrete.

## Start with the existing experience

Explain your coaching offer, who it is for and where interest currently comes from. Share the page a prospective client sees, the steps they take to enquire, and the communications they receive afterwards.

If you have visitor, enquiry or booking numbers, share the period and source they cover. Where information is missing, acknowledge it. A useful review does not require pretending every step has already been measured.

## Define the work

A proposal should make the deliverables understandable. For example:

- Which landing page or service pages will be written and rebuilt?
- How will someone select the appropriate service or consultation?
- What qualification questions are useful?
- Which booking or contact tools will be connected?
- Which confirmations, preparation messages and reminders are included?
- Who supplies approved credentials, images and testimonials?

Single-offer and multi-service coaching businesses can need different structures. The agreed scope should reflect that distinction.

## Agree how decisions will be made

Discuss who provides feedback, who approves the copy and design, and how changes to the scope will be handled. Make the review process clear before the build starts.

## Understand the commercial terms

Ask for a clear explanation of the price, payment arrangements, responsibilities and any ongoing work. Evaluate these alongside the actual deliverables and the work involved. A payment structure on its own does not establish the quality or suitability of a project.

## Decide what to review after launch

Use measures that are appropriate to your business and the available evidence. An improved enquiry path may be assessed through qualified enquiries, booking completion or feedback from prospective clients. Small samples need careful interpretation.

Keep traffic sources and changes to the coaching offer in mind. A website rebuild does not create traffic by itself, and no individual page can guarantee a particular commercial outcome.
