# Homepage evidence

- **Mobile traffic.** Mobile accounted for 81% of commercial and professional services landing-page visits in Unbounce’s 2024 report. It describes the device mix in that sample. The dataset covers July 2023 to July 2024. [Industry results](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/) · [Methodology](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/methodology/)
- **Form usability.** Seckler et al. tested three original forms against redesigned versions in a controlled experiment with 65 people. Successful first attempts rose for all three forms. Average completion time fell 25% and 33% for the two forms with statistically significant time improvements. See Tables 3 and 6. [CHI 2014 paper](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-research2023-media/pubtools/pdf/42513.pdf)
