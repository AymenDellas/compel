# Compel public website

## Current direction

A clear, conversational studio website. The copy explains what Compel builds and what that means for an established coaching business. No vague section slogans, fictional visitors, essay interface, labels above headings or visible research citations.

## Visual contract

- Matte charcoal `#121510`, warm-white text `#f2f5eb` and Compel lime `#b1ff00`. Every section shares the same canvas. Meaningful phrases receive bold weight or a lime highlight with dark text.
- The supplied Focura reference informs the hero background only: two broad, tapered, softly blurred lime light streaks enter diagonally from the edges. Built in CSS, with a clear center for the headline. No background image, UI mockup or generated artwork.
- The navbar is one compact rounded charcoal container containing exactly the wordmark and review action. No links, menu or secondary text. The keyboard skip link remains outside the container.
- The hero and opening sections educate before describing the service. The first Compel service pitch is in the final service section, followed by implementation and the review invitation. Only the standard navbar CTA appears before that invitation.
- The centered hero introduces the question in plain English. Manrope carries the interface; Instrument Serif adds selective emphasis to the hero and final invitation.
- The main action is a warm-white button with a lime circular arrow inset. The compact navbar action uses lime. Hover only changes the surface color. No movement, scaling, shimmer, glow, magnetic tracking or animated arrow.
- All content sections use one vertical column, with an 800px maximum width. The first section after the hero is a single paragraph. The research finding is integrated into the copy on the same background. Service headings and descriptions stack. There are no side-by-side text layouts or oversized number blocks.
- Headlines are smaller: 62px for the desktop hero, up to 40px for content headings and 30px for mobile content headings. The copy has been rewritten as a confident, practical explanation.
- The 65-person study remains accurately described inline. Its visible attribution and disclosure are removed as requested; verification stays in `content/home-research.md`. The count is not a conversion percentage or business result.
- Public routes use the same matte-and-lime palette. Private proposal styling and functionality stay independent. Legal and business links remain in the compact colophon.

## Verify

Inspect the page at 1440, 1024 and 390px. Check beam placement, text contrast and highlights, the two-item navbar, static button feedback, and the inquiry form's focus, validation and close behavior. Run the production build and existing lead/proposal tests. Do not submit a real lead during visual QA.
